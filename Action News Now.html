WILLOWS, Calif. - A traffic stop in Willows led to a drug and weapons arrest after a driver was found to be under the influence and carrying a concealed firearm.

The California Highway Patrol says they pulled over a suspected drunk driver, who was later determined to be under the influence of drugs. During the stop, officers say they discovered a concealed pistol on the driver.

Under California law, possessing a controlled substance and a firearm at the same time is a felony. A conviction can carry up to 4 years in prison and a $10,000 fine.

Authorities say the driver was arrested following the stop.

TfGM lifts overnight parking fees across 41 park and ride sites for Manchester Christmas Markets; visitors can now leave their cars for free and collect them the next day

More than 7,500 free spaces at dozens of sites sit next to tram, bus or train services, as TfGM expects around 3.4 million extra visitors during the Christmas Markets period alone.

If you’re planning to drive into Manchester for the Christmas Markets, a shopping trip or a night out, you can leave the car on the edge of the city and let public transport do the rest. Transport for Greater Manchester (TfGM) is encouraging those heading into the city to use one of the region’s 41 park and ride sites offering 6,610 free spaces, including 319 disabled bays, then take the tram, bus or train.

Plus, TfGM has confirmed that overnight restrictions have also been lifted. You can head out for the evening and collect your car the next day without being charged.

About 3.4 million extra visitors are expected, so TfGM’s Chief Network Officer Danny Vaughan urges drivers to plan ahead.

Where the park and ride spaces are in Greater Manchester

Metrolink: 16 sites with 3,600+ spaces, the largest being Radcliffe (458), Parkway (341, handy for the Trafford Centre and Old Trafford), Whitefield (318), Sale Water Park (285) and East Didsbury (278).

Bus and rail: 800+ bus spaces at Hazel Grove (400) and the Leigh Guided Busway stops at Wardley (259), Leigh (131) and Tyldesley (46), with 21 local railway stations adding over 2,300 more.

Weekend pop-up sites: 650+ extra free spaces, weekends from November 14 to Christmas, 9am to 8pm:

  • Bury Grammar School for Girls, Bridge Road, Bury
  • St Monica’s High School, Bury Old Road, Prestwich
  • Emirates Old Trafford, Talbot Road, Trafford
  • Bowker Vale Primary School, Middleton Road, Manchester
  • St Edmunds RC Primary School, Upper Monsall Street, Manchester
  • University of Manchester car park, Booth Street (from Sunday 22 November)

Full details are on the Bee Network website.

Other festive transport changes

Also planned:

  • More double trams and event services
  • New night buses in all 10 boroughs
  • Direct bus and tram links to the Trafford Centre
  • A longer half-hourly 615 (Bolton to Middlebrook)
  • Extra staff at busy tram stops
  • Control-centre monitoring of the network

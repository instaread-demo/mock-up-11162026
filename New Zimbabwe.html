‘Pasi NeZvigananda’ slogan out of step, let’s honour our middle class— Zanu PF war veterans leader Mahiya

By: New Zimbabwe
Date:15th November 2026

ZANU PF war veterans secretary Douglas Mahiya has called on Zimbabweans to shun the chant “Pasi neZvigananda”, arguing it no longer fits a nation trying to grow its own business class.

The term ‘Zvigananda’ was popularised by Vice President Constantino Chiwenga who has been on a sustained attack on the monied few allegedly riding on their proximity to powerful politicians to get State tenders.

Speaking as guest of honour at the ZANU PF Mashonaland West Provincial Coordinating Committee (PCC) meeting in Chinhoyi on Saturday, Mahiya said the term belonged to a different era.

Mahiya said the word once described wealthy backers of the Ian Smith regime and argued it does not fit today’s local entrepreneurs.

Some opposition voices apply the label to ZANU PF-aligned businesspeople such as Kuda Tagwireyi and Paul Tungwarara; Mahiya called them central to economic recovery.

Citing Vision 2030, he urged Zimbabweans to applaud wealth creators and the middle class.

Weekend Box Office: Ebenezer Wins a Sleepy Weekend

Meanwhile, David Leitch's How to Rob a Bank stalls in seventh place, and Godzilla take s a tumble.

By Erik Childress | Published November 15, 2026

TAGGED AS: Box Office, movies, news

It was certainly a weekend of holds and drops. Some solid formers for films finding their audience and a big latter for the big lizard not easing the trauma of WWII. Audiences were not overly enthused with this week’s new offerings and while one modestly led the box office they may be saving their money for the next month worth of titles.

King of the Crop: Ebenezer Wins a Sleepy Weekend

Watch on

Ti West’s Johnny Depp-led take on Scrooge, Ebenezer, topped the chart for Paramount with $19 million, below the starts of Robert Zemeckis’ A Christmas Carol ($30.01 million) and, adjusted for inflation, Scrooged (about $37 million). That is a workable figure for a $38 million production. It is Depp’s first notable lead since the last Pirates of the Caribbean in 2017; Black Mass opened to $22.6 million and ended above $62 million. Overseas added $23 million.

Tales of the Top 10: The Cat in the Hat Takes Down Godzilla

Watch on

The Cat in the Hat took $11.4 million in weekend two for $38.2 million in 10 days. Its pace resembles WB’s Happy Feet Two ($43.7 million after 10 days), pointing to a $60-70 million domestic finish and a disappointment for the studio.

Godzilla Minus Zero dropped 73% to $8.3 million ($48.8 million in 10 days), nearly matching Street Fighter’s run, though it reportedly cost $18 million versus $82 million and has passed $80 million worldwide.

Watch on

Indian releases usually fall steeply in their second weekend (RRR lost 83%), but Ramayana: Rise of a Legend eased just 38% to $5.5 million. Sony’s release has $24.1 million and is set to become the top Indian grosser ever in North America.

Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim’s documentary You Can See Everything added $5.4 million in weekend five for $89 million in 31 days, on track for $100-105 million and already ahead of Mortal Kombat II, Supergirl and Street Fighter.

Watch on

Sony’s Ghost Soldier, with Jim Caviezel, opened to $5.2 million ($8.3 million since Veteran’s Day), the studio’s third sub-$10 million start this year for a film in 2,500+ theaters.

Amazon/MGM’s How to Rob a Bank, David Leitch’s film with Nicholas Hoult and Zoë Kravitz moved from Labor Day, opened to only $5.1 million against a reported $60 million budget, the studio’s weakest opening this year in 2,000+ theaters. Peter Farrelly’s I Play Rocky made $504,000 in 29 theaters ($17,373 average) and $954,000 in 10 days.

Watch on

Searchlight’s Wild Horse Nine slipped only 22.2% to $4.6 million ($13.3 million so far, $28 million worldwide). Laika’s Wildwood added $4.5 million for $48.4 million in 24 days, heading toward the studio’s second-best domestic total, though it has just $70 million worldwide on a $100 million budget.

WB and DC’s Clayface earned $3.7 million in weekend four for $63.6 million, $6.3 million behind Supergirl at this stage and bound for about $70 million. Its $121 million worldwide makes it DC’s sixth release since 2020 below $200 million globally.

Beyond the Top 10: Drummer Boy Drops Out, Victorian Psycho Doesn’t Make the Cut

Watch on

Angel Studios’ Drummer Boy fell 62.9% to $3.5 million ($16.1 million in 10 days) and may land near $20 million. Black Bear’s Wicker reached $23.3 million, while Bleecker Street’s Victorian Psycho opened to $1.08 million in 1,200 theaters.

Neon’s Paper Tiger from James Gray took $215,000 in six theaters. A24 widened Club Kid to 27 theaters for $756,000 (over $1.4 million total), and Netflix’s La bola Negra made $500,000 in 341 theaters for $3.5 million.

On The Vine: The Hunger Games Return

Watch on

Lionsgate’s The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping leads next week. Limited openers include Focus’ Elsinore with Andrew Scott (89%), Channing Tatum in the Certified Fresh Josephine, Nicolas Cage in Best Pancakes In the County and John Travolta in November 1963. See all upcoming releases here.

Full List of Box Office Results: November 13-15

  1. Ebenezer – $19.0 million ($19.0 million total)
  2. The Cat in the Hat – $11.4 million ($38.2 million total)
  3. Godzilla Minus Zero – $8.3 million ($48.8 million total)
  4. Ramayana: Rise of a Legend – $5.5 million ($24.1 million total)
  5. You Can See Everything – $5.4 million ($89.0 million total)
  6. Ghost Soldier – $5.2 million ($8.3 million total)
  7. How to Rob a Bank – $5.1 million ($5.1 million total)
  8. Wild Horse Nine – $4.6 million ($9.0 million total)
  9. Wildwood – $4.5 million ($48.4 million total)
  10. Clayface – $3.7 million ($63.6 million total)

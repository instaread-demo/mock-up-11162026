One of the most delightful things about London every winter is seeing the capital fill with Christmas lights. Shopfronts are transformed, and fairy lights shine down from above throughout the evenings. So what if you combined twinkling Christmas displays with London’s tallest building? Well, that’s exactly what’s happening this December, when the Shard will transform into the highest Advent calendar in the city.

From 1 December, a new design is unveiled at 4pm daily, replaying every 15 minutes as a numbered door of light opens.

Last year’s Christmas display at the Shard drew on housekeeper Gustavo Zuluaga Villegas’s art; now occupiers, staff and families have co-created each design.

The Shard Advent calendar

Covering the top 20 floors of the 310-metre tower, it should be visible 40 miles away.

Staff children designed it with artist Kinga Szulakowska, who also runs housekeeping at Shangri-La The Shard.

Kinga Szulakowska calls it “a real pleasure”, adding that the community’s work being seen citywide makes it special.

The animations continue from 25 December to 5 January, over the Twelve Days of Christmas.
